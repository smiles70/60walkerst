# Hero High-Impact Redesign Epic

## Objective
Transform the hero section from a functional C+ to a visually striking, high-converting, modern design that exceeds in all 10 audit criteria.

## Success Criteria
All 10 Design Agent audit criteria graded ✅ (exceeding high-impact standards).

## Sprints (Concurrent Where Possible)

### Sprint 1: Typography & Composition Lockup
- Centered dramatic typographic lockup
- Headline dominates with tight line-height
- Address secondary, tagline whispers
- Generous breathing room (px-8+, py-16+)

### Sprint 2: Button Personality Redesign
- Distinctive button treatment (not generic Tailwind pills)
- Stronger shadow layering
- Border treatment with personality
- Magnetic visual weight

### Sprint 3: Gradient & Emotional Mood
- Warm gradient that enhances (not washes out) the door photo
- Preserve the invitation/welcome feeling
- Darken edges, not center

### Sprint 4: Motion & Entrance Choreography
- Subtle staggered entrance animation on load
- Headline → subtitle → buttons sequence
- CSS-only, no JS animation library

### Sprint 5: Mobile Optimization
- Thumb-zone button placement
- Vertical stack with confidence
- Larger tap targets, generous spacing

## Quality Gates
- build, test, lint, typecheck, slop, security — all pass per sprint
- Zero bugs before commit
